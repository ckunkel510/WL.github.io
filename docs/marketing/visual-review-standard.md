# Visual review before email release

Every campaign must receive a visual walkthrough of the exact saved HTML before owner approval and release. The daily review task must include desktop and mobile screenshots, concise findings and a verified action map. A machine link check is insufficient. The approval policy now requires a passed visual report bound to the HTML SHA-256 and creative revision, with both screenshots and all checks below. A changed creative invalidates that report and the exact owner approval. This does not authorize sending or activate the incomplete production sender.

Inspect the first screen, every product row, transitions and the entire footer in the saved Constant Contact desktop/mobile preview. Check campaign specificity, product scale, readable type/contrast, full product crops, tap targets, mobile order, CTA ownership, exact image/title/link correspondence, supported/native preference tags and native unsubscribe/address/footer. Review the full scroll as a visual composition. Reduce repeated explanatory, brand and preference blocks to one concise relationship close. Keep any model-fit, battery, accessory, offer and fulfillment qualifiers customers need.

Avoid more than two consecutive copy-led sections. Use larger exact-product images, a concise comparison/spec rail, an asymmetric feature, editorial price banner or another deliberate visual reset. Do not substitute a new plain rectangle and paragraph for each old rectangle. All exact products must remain identifiable and linked. No synthetic product/lifestyle claims.

STIHL editions use the official unmodified wordmark, orange/white/charcoal visual language and task-led equipment imagery, with Woodson and Built on Quality still visible. Preserve logo proportions and clear space. Keep Lexington, Groesbeck and Mexia targeting and dealer-confirmed fulfillment. Other categories use brand treatments appropriate to their own products and customer jobs; do not apply STIHL styling to unrelated mail.

November18 creative revision2 retains the same products, subjects, public campaign/link IDs and unsent native campaign IDs; the HTML and visual approval need fresh review. Existing audiences remain proposals. `reviseMorningDraftsVisually` preflights all three exact old/new source hashes, draft state, metadata and absence of recipients before writing; it preserves sender and physical footer, verifies exact saved content and cannot send, schedule or select recipients. Unknown owner edits or sent campaigns stop the helper before any writes.

Review screenshot evidence and the report record are assertions by a trusted reviewing adapter. The policy cannot establish aesthetic quality from booleans alone; do the actual visual work. Screenshots are not client compatibility guarantees—native desktop/mobile previews cover those renderers; Outlook uses a safe natural-image-height fallback. Continue to require owner approval of creative, audience and timing.
